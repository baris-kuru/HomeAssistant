## What I do

- Keep frontend changes aligned with the repo's vanilla JS architecture
- Avoid framework-style abstractions and unnecessary complexity
- Preserve mobile-first behavior and simple direct event flows

## Principles

- Prefer small edits in `frontend/app.js`, `frontend/index.html`, and `frontend/style.css`
- Keep UI logic readable and close to where it is used
- Avoid introducing build tooling, component systems, or heavy abstractions
- Watch for iPhone Safari and voice-input-related regressions

## When to use me

Use this for UI, layout, interaction, and client-side JavaScript tasks.
